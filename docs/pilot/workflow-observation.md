## Trace five representative orders

For each order record source, acceptance, item routing, preparation, assembly, quality check, packing/service, payment/settlement, completion, exceptions, handoffs, elapsed time, and duplicate data entry. Include at least one modification, cancellation, stockout, delayed item, and connectivity failure.

## Station map

Record each station’s inputs, outputs, capacity constraint, equipment, skill requirements, allergens, cleaning controls, queue ownership, escalation rule, and current source of truth.

## Inventory and production

Trace one ingredient from purchase request through receiving, storage, preparation yield, recipe consumption, waste, count, variance, invoice matching, and supplier settlement. Record units and conversions at every handoff.

## Failure and recovery

- WAN unavailable
- Power or device failure
- Printer or KDS unavailable
- Marketplace connector unavailable
- Duplicate or missing order
- Ingredient unavailable after acceptance
- Payment/tender disagreement
- Allergen or food-safety concern

For each, capture what staff do now, who decides, what evidence survives, and how reconciliation occurs.

## Validation

The outlet manager and at least one frontline worker review the resulting workflow map. Product assumptions not observed directly are labelled hypotheses.
