# Canonical domain and data contracts

Status: Normative Phase 0 contract

## Organization hierarchy

```text
Organization
  -> Legal Entity
    -> Property or Cluster
      -> Outlet
        -> Revenue Centre
          -> Brand
            -> Kitchen
              -> Station or Storage Location
```

An entity may be omitted only where the business does not use that level; APIs still expose the resolved ancestor path. `organization_id` is the tenant identifier. Access grants target a node plus a scope (`self`, `descendants`, or an explicit set).

`Brand` is organization-owned, while an active `BrandOutletAssignment` makes that brand operational at a particular outlet. This is the controlled rollout boundary for virtual brands: creating a brand never exposes it to every kitchen, channel, or POS automatically. The assignment is effective immediately only for the selected outlet's configuration; its live POS, KDS, printers, and edge runtime stay bound to that physical outlet.

## SaaS operator provisioning

FeastCloud staff administer the platform through a separate `platform_admin` role. The platform onboarding command atomically creates one customer tenant, its organization root, first outlet, first active brand deployment, and a template station topology. Restaurant `owner` and `manager` roles cannot call this command or read another tenant.

The command records the intended customer owner and returns `identity_invite_pending`. Identity activation and email delivery are delegated to Keycloak; a customer must not be shown as live until that adapter is configured and its invitation has been delivered. This avoids pretending that a plain email field is an account.

All identifiers are UUIDv7 strings generated by the mutation origin, including offline clients. They are opaque to users and never reused. Human identifiers such as order numbers are separately scoped, configurable, and not primary keys.

## Shared value objects

| Value | Contract |
|---|---|
| Money | `{ "minorUnits": int64, "currency": "ISO-4217" }`; never binary floating point |
| Quantity | `{ "value": "decimal-string", "unit_id": UUID, "scale": int }`; conversions cite `conversion_id` and version |
| Local business time | UTC instant plus IANA timezone; business date is derived using the outlet's versioned trading-day policy |
| Effective period | Half-open `[effective_from, effective_to)`; null end means current; overlapping active versions are rejected |
| Address | Country-pack-defined structured fields plus optional formatted value; no global mandatory state/postcode assumption |
| Translation | Stable message/content key, BCP 47 locale, value, pack version, review state, and optional fallback locale |
| Percentage/rate | Decimal string with an explicit basis; never infer whether `10` means 10% or 0.10 |

Quantities MUST remain in their recorded unit. Derived base-unit quantities store the conversion provenance and rounding result. Monetary rounding occurs only at country-pack-defined boundaries and records the rule version.

## Mutation context

Every mutation produces or carries this context:

```json
{
  "id": "uuidv7-operation-id",
  "tenantId": "uuidv7",
  "outletId": "uuidv7",
  "deviceId": "uuidv7",
  "actorId": "uuidv7-or-service-principal",
  "occurredAt": "RFC3339Nano",
  "source": "feastcloud-edge",
  "sourceId": "source-stable-id",
  "schemaVersion": "1.0",
  "idempotencyKey": "opaque-unique-key",
  "correlationId": "uuidv7",
  "causationId": "uuidv7-or-null",
  "payload": {}
}
```

This is the public camelCase envelope defined by `packages/contracts/schemas/mutation-envelope.json`. An organization-scoped cloud command uses its registered service/device identity and an explicitly authorized organization outlet scope; omission is not used to bypass scope validation. The server derives and validates tenant, actor, and permitted outlet from authentication. `recordedAt` is assigned by the accepting authority. `occurredAt` may reflect an offline device clock and cannot establish authorization or sequence. Database columns may use snake_case but cannot change the public wire contract.

## Canonical entity families

### Catalog

- `Ingredient`, `Unit`, `UnitConversion`, `Allergen`, `RecipeVersion`, `RecipeComponent`, `Yield`, `MenuItemVersion`, `Modifier`, and `PriceVersion`.
- Recipe and menu versions are immutable after use. A sale captures the exact item, recipe, price, tax, and allergen-policy version used.
- A recipe graph MUST be acyclic for its effective period. Unit dimensions must be compatible, and yield must be positive.

### Commerce and kitchen

- `Order`, `OrderLine`, `Fulfillment`, `Tender`, `Refund`, `TaxDocument`, `Settlement`, `KitchenTicket`, `StationTask`, `PreparationBatch`, and `QualityCheck`.
- An order line captures item name, quantity, modifiers, price, taxes, destination station set, and recipe version so later catalog edits do not rewrite history.
- Order and ticket state transitions are explicit commands with transition records. Cancellation and refund are separate concepts.
- A tender records only tokenized/provider references and display-safe metadata. PAN and CVV are prohibited.

### Supply and production

- `InventoryEvent`, `InventoryCount`, `CostLayer`, `WasteEvent`, `Lot`, `Transfer`, `Supplier`, `Quotation`, `PurchaseOrder`, `GoodsReceipt`, and `SupplierInvoice`.
- A stock balance is a projection over immutable inventory events, scoped by tenant, storage location, ingredient, lot, and optional cost layer.
- Counts do not replace balances. Closing an approved count emits a variance event whose evidence references the observed count and prior projection.
- Receipts, transfers, preparation yields, consumption, waste, spoilage, staff meals, and corrections use distinct reason codes.

### People, guest, and intelligence

- `Employee`, `Role`, `Grant`, `Skill`, `Certification`, `Schedule`, `Shift`, and `AttendanceEvent`.
- `GuestProfile`, `ConsentRecord`, `Preference`, `Reservation`, `LoyaltyAccount`, and `Communication` keep consent purpose, source, effective time, and withdrawal history.
- `Forecast`, `Recommendation`, `Evidence`, `Approval`, `Decision`, `Outcome`, and `ModelVersion` make every assisted decision attributable and measurable.

## Ledger contract

Every inventory, cash, tender, or financial ledger row contains:

- Immutable event ID and mutation context.
- Account or stock dimensions, signed amount/quantity, reason code, and effective business date.
- Source document type and ID, plus an optional `reversal_of_event_id`.
- Currency or unit and any conversion/rounding provenance.
- Integrity checksum over canonicalized business fields.

Ledger tables deny `UPDATE` and `DELETE` to runtime roles. A correction appends an equal-and-opposite reversal and, when needed, a replacement. The database enforces uniqueness of `(tenant_id, source, source_id, event_kind)`. A projection checkpoint records its last consumed ledger event and can be rebuilt and compared with the live projection.

## Domain-event envelope

Internal events are immutable facts named in past tense and carried in a CloudEvents-compatible envelope:

```json
{
  "specversion": "1.0",
  "id": "uuidv7",
  "source": "/tenants/{tenant}/modules/commerce",
  "type": "com.feastcloud.commerce.order.accepted.v1",
  "subject": "orders/{order_id}",
  "time": "RFC3339Nano",
  "datacontenttype": "application/json",
  "dataschema": "https://schemas.feastcloud.org/events/order-accepted/v1",
  "tenantid": "uuidv7",
  "correlationid": "uuidv7",
  "causationid": "uuidv7",
  "data": {}
}
```

Consumers MUST ignore unknown additive fields, reject unsupported major schema versions, deduplicate by event ID, and persist their inbox record atomically with their business effect. Event payloads contain identifiers and the minimum required data, not unrestricted entity snapshots or secrets.

## Audit and retention invariants

- Security, approval, configuration, export, and privileged-repair actions create audit events recording actor, scope, reason, before/after references, and correlation ID.
- Audit records and ledgers are append-only. Access to their payloads is itself audited.
- Retention and deletion come from the effective country pack and customer policy. A privacy deletion anonymizes or cryptographically erases eligible personal fields without deleting legally retained financial facts.
- Voice audio is opt-in, purpose-bound, and disabled from long-term storage by default. Transcripts follow the retention class of the workflow they serve.
